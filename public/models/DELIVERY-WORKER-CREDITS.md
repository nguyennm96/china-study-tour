The local GLB is the original decompressed asset. Runtime adaptations: normal smoothing, orange/navy material palette, scene scale/orientation, authored Idle_Neutral and Walk skeletal animations, and an IK overlay for holding the parcel. The walk is sampled by accumulated travel distance. After releasing the parcel, the worker turns 180 degrees in place and walks forward away from the drone. Foot height is grounded on the rooftop, and the carrying arms use a fixed elbow pole. No manufacturer, employee or endorsement claim is made.
